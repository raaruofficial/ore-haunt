# ORE HAUNT — Demo Video Script (CLOCK IN)

**Runtime target:** 75–90 seconds · **Format:** portrait screen recording on a Seeker (or 412×915 capture), app audio off, voiceover recorded clean.
**Director's notes:** Record the real app — no mockups, no sped-up data. Let the replay play at natural speed; cut *between* screens, never inside a number. Silence notifications before recording. Anything the narration doesn't claim, the screen doesn't imply: no balances you don't own, no completed payment — the checkout shot ends at the wallet approval sheet.

---

### Shot 1 — Cold open: the round replays itself · 0:00–0:09

- **On screen:** App boots straight into a cinematic replay already in motion — the 5×5 tile board filling deploy-by-deploy, telemetry ticking, the round info card below.
- **Narration:** "Every ORE round is public. Every deploy, every tile, every wallet — written on-chain. The problem was never the data. It's that nobody could read it."
- **Judges should notice:** No menu-diving — the product opens on its best surface. This is real recorded round data with real timestamps, not an animation of fake numbers.

### Shot 2 — The replay, up close · 0:09–0:18

- **On screen:** Stay in the player. The board completes; the winning tile lands; the info card shows the round's full record — pot, miners, winner, timing.
- **Narration:** "ORE HAUNT rebuilds finished rounds deploy-by-deploy, so you can watch how a round actually played out — not just who won."
- **Judges should notice:** Telemetry + board + info card in one screen, in that order. The replay engine is a separate, stable module — it plays recorded timelines exactly as they happened.

### Shot 3 — Last 4 hauntings · 0:18–0:28

- **On screen:** Back to the REPLAY tab. The **Last 4 hauntings** grid: four replay cards on one page, each with its full summary, and above it the live timer — "LAST REPLAY · ENDED … AGO" — ticking.
- **Narration:** "The last four rounds sit on one page, full information, no tapping around. And up top, a live timer tells you how long ago the last round ended."
- **Judges should notice:** The timer is computed live from the newest replay's actual closing timestamp. Four complete round summaries are visible at once — the page is deliberately light: static cards, no engines running until you press play.

### Shot 4 — MINERS: the dossier · 0:28–0:43

- **On screen:** MINERS tab → the 150-miner leaderboard → tap a top miner. The dossier opens: tile fingerprint heatmap, strategy classification, lifetime stats. Scroll to the **Anomaly exposure** card.
- **Narration:** "One hundred and fifty miners, each with a dossier — where they deploy, how they play, what their fingerprint looks like. And where a miner keeps showing up in flagged rounds, the dossier says so — and how often."
- **Judges should notice:** Pick a miner who *has* an exposure card (the current #1 does). The card's own copy makes the honest distinction: presence in flagged rounds is a fact about deployments — it says nothing about the draw, which is uniform. That honesty is a design rule in this app.

### Shot 5 — ROUNDS explorer · 0:43–0:53

- **On screen:** ROUNDS tab → scroll the 200-round explorer → open one round detail: the 25-tile board, pot, winner, the round's full breakdown.
- **Narration:** "Two hundred rounds of history, searchable and sortable — every round's board exactly as it settled. Anomalies are flagged where the pattern breaks: twenty-eight signals across the window."
- **Judges should notice:** The explorer, the stats, and the anomaly layer are the same verified dataset as the replays — one derivation, every door. Round times here are labeled estimates where they're estimated; replay times are exact.

### Shot 6 — GHOST: the companion · 0:53–1:01

- **On screen:** GHOST tab. The mining-ghost companion — its look derived from the miner profile — with its level and XP.
- **Narration:** "And because this lives on a Seeker, your .skr has a ghost. It hatches from your own mining profile and grows as you explore. A companion — not a score, not a promise."
- **Judges should notice:** Identity-flavored but honest: in this milestone the companion is a local prototype — it never claims to be authenticated identity.

### Shot 7 — Checkout: pay with what you mine · 1:01–1:16

- **On screen:** Open the plans/checkout. Show the tiers. Flip the currency toggle from **SOL to ORE** — "Pay with what you mine" — then on to **SKR**, and **hold on SKR for ~4s** (same hold as ORE). Flip back to a tier currency, pick a tier, hit pay: the Solana Pay approval sheet opens from the wallet. **Hold on the sheet — do not complete a real payment on camera.**
- **Narration:** "Checkout is built in — day, monthly, lifetime, and a capped anomaly tier. You can pay in SOL, in ORE — pay with what you mine — or in SKR, the Seeker ecosystem's own token. For this demo milestone, checkout is pointed at my own wallet — the rail is real, the store launch comes next."
- **Judges should notice:** The toggle is the product idea in miniature — miners earn ORE in the protocol and spend it in the intel layer, and SKR rides the same verified rail. Verification checks the treasury's on-chain balance delta; nothing unlocks on a promise. The anomaly tier is capped at 100 slots per rolling 24 hours.

### Shot 8 — Close · 1:16–1:25

- **On screen:** Cut back to a replay board mid-play, then fade to the title card: **ORE HAUNT** — "Know the miners. Read the rounds." — *Built for Seeker · raaru.skr*
- **Narration:** "ORE HAUNT. Know the miners. Read the rounds."
- **Judges should notice:** The whole product in one line — it's an intel tool. It shows you the record. What you do with it is yours.

**Total: ≈ 1:25.**

## Claims discipline (do not improvise past this list)

- **Never on camera:** earnings, profit, ROI, "edge," predictions, winner-picking, price talk. The app describes deployments and history — nothing about what a round *will* do.
- **Checkout honesty line is mandatory** in Shot 7 — do not cut it for time; cut Shot 6 instead if the take runs long.
- **Companion honesty:** "local prototype" framing stays.
- Numbers allowed (all verified): 150 miners · 200 rounds · 8 replays · 28 anomaly signals · 100 anomaly slots, capped per rolling 24h · tiers $1 / $5 / $50 / $7.
