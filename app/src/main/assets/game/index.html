How winners are picked

Each round, the ORE program takes four random numbers from the blockchain, mixes them together, and the remainder after dividing by 25 picks the winning tile. Nobody — not even the biggest miner — can predict or change it.

winner = (r1 ^ r2 ^ r3 ^ r4) % 25
What we estimate

Replay times are real on-chain timestamps. Dates in the round explorer are estimated from round spacing (~63s per round) and marked with ~. Close, not exact.

Limits

Round accounts recycle about a day after close — history from before collection started is unrecoverable. Nothing here is financial advice.
